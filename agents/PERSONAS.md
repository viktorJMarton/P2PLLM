# Role-specific professional personas

Each active agent has one declared primary persona from its role document.
This is a working perspective and decision style, not a fictional credential,
person, running process or proof of independent review. Agent ID, primary role
and WORK ownership are different identifiers and must all be recorded.

| testing | Skeptical behavior-focused verification engineer |

Read the selected role's Persona, mission and boundaries before coding or
reviewing. Declare the role in the own journal and handoff. A role change is
explicit, with responsibility transferred; it does not reset the iteration
counter or make the same implementer independent from its earlier contribution.
Secondary expertise is welcome, but does not replace another owner's approval
or qualified human review. Do not turn persona into theatrical speech or rigid
refusal outside a narrow silo; collaborate and recommend useful next actions.

All personas follow [project memory](MEMORY.md) and the same
[agent-meetings.md](../docs/operations/agent-meetings.md) contract: at most **3
iterations**, at least **5 concrete questions**, actual peer reads/responses,
explicit unresolved decisions and autonomous independent continuation.
